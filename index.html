About SARAN & Independent App Notice

SARAN – Go Anywhere ("SARAN", "we", "us", or "our") is a public transit journey-planning and navigation mobile application published by Saran Mobility (Android package: com.sarandev.transitapp) designed to help passengers explore and navigate bus, metro, suburban rail, tram, and ferry networks.

Non-Government Affiliation Disclaimer

SARAN is an independent application. It is not affiliated with, authorized by, endorsed by, or operated by the Government of India, Indian Railways, or any state road transport corporation or government transit agency. To view all official open data sources and verification links, visit our dedicated Data Sources & Disclaimer page.

Guest access first: You can use SARAN completely without creating an account or signing in. Offline timetable searches, route exploration, and nearby stop calculations do not require authentication. Creating an account is entirely optional and serves only to synchronize bookmarks across devices.

1. Information We Process

The information processed by SARAN depends strictly on the features you choose to use:

  • Optional account & profile information: If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In or email registration, we process your email address, display name, and unique user identifier. Optional profile details (such as commute preferences) are processed only if voluntarily configured.
  • Journey and search queries: The origins, destinations, transit mode filters, and departure times you select to plan a trip. Search queries entered during guest mode are processed locally or ephemerally and are not linked to your personal identity.
  • Location information: Approximate or precise device location if you grant location permission, as well as stops or addresses you manually type. (See Section 3 for full details).
  • Saved places and bookmarks: Favorite bus lines, metro stations, home/work locations, and custom place tags you save. For guest users, these remain strictly on your local device. For signed-in users, they are synced to your secure cloud profile.
  • User-submitted error reports & feedback: Transit schedule corrections, missing stop reports, or feedback voluntarily sent to us, including any screenshot or message details you attach.
  • Usage, diagnostic, and technical data: App version, operating system release, device model, performance logs, and anonymized crash diagnostics collected via Google Firebase to monitor app stability.
  • Advertising ID (AAID): If advertising or analytics SDKs access the Android Advertising Identifier, it is handled strictly in compliance with Google Play Developer policies and user device settings. SARAN does not sell Advertising IDs or use them for cross-app surveillance.

2. How We Use Information

We process information exclusively to provide, maintain, and enhance SARAN's transit features, specifically to:

  • Authenticate sign-ins and synchronize saved bookmarks across devices when an optional account is created.
  • Compute optimal transit routes, transfer connections, walking segments, and timetable schedules.
  • Display nearby bus stops, metro stations, and train platforms relative to your current location.
  • Provide active turn-by-turn navigation guidance, stop arrival reminders, and trip progress alerts.
  • Investigate crashes, monitor performance, and improve routing algorithms across transport networks.
  • Respond to user-submitted data corrections, customer support requests, and account deletion requests.

We do not sell personal data: Saran Mobility does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, search history, or location coordinates to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

3. Location Permissions & Navigation GPS Processing

Location access in SARAN is governed by strict privacy controls and transparency:

  • Foreground location permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): When granted, SARAN accesses your location while the app is actively on-screen to pinpoint your location on the map, list nearby stations, and initiate route searches from your current location. You can deny this permission and manually type departure points instead.
  • Foreground navigation service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION): When you explicitly start active trip navigation, SARAN initiates an active foreground service with an ongoing, prominent system notification (e.g. "Navigating route to destination — 3 stops remaining"). This allows the app to update your upcoming stop alerts and vibration reminders even if you lock your screen or switch apps during your commute.
  • Local on-device processing: Your real-time navigation GPS coordinates are calculated and evaluated locally on your device by the offline routing engine. SARAN does not stream, broadcast, or log your live GPS coordinates to external servers.
  • No covert 24/7 background tracking: SARAN does not track your location silently in the background when you are not actively navigating a trip or using the app. Location services immediately terminate when you finish or cancel your navigation session.

4. Offline Transit Databases & Cloud Infrastructure

SARAN is engineered with an offline-first architecture to ensure reliable navigation even during cellular dead zones:

  • Local SQLite transit databases: Timetable schedules, stop coordinates, and routing matrices are packaged or downloaded as compressed local SQLite databases stored directly in private app storage. Searching schedules and routing offline occurs completely on-device without network communication.
  • Cloud backend services: Where cloud synchronization is active (such as optional user profile management and bookmark backup), SARAN utilizes secure backend infrastructure (such as Cloudflare serverless services and Turso database clusters). Transmissions are encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

5. Service Providers & Third-Party Services

To deliver specific technical features, SARAN utilizes trusted third-party service providers who process limited data under industry-standard security practices:

  • Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics: Collects anonymized usage telemetry (such as screen views, route calculation latency, and crash stack traces) to maintain app reliability. Google's privacy practices are governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Sign-In: Facilitates secure, one-tap identity verification if you choose to authenticate using your Google Account.
  • Cloud Infrastructure (Cloudflare / Turso): Hosts secure backend APIs, static assets, and encrypted cloud database records for signed-in user profiles.
  • Map & Geocoding Providers: Map tiles and open geocoding lookups (such as OpenStreetMap contributors) may process search keywords or tile requests necessary to render visual cartography.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Guest users: No account profile is created. Search queries and temporary route calculations are discarded after use. Locally stored bookmarks, search history, and downloaded offline timetable packages can be purged immediately by selecting Clear Data / Storage in your Android device settings.
  • Account holders: If you created an optional account, your account records and cloud-synced bookmarks are retained while your account remains active.
  • How to delete your account: You can delete your account at any time through the in-app Delete Account option located in app settings, or submit an external request on our public Account Deletion Portal without needing the app installed.
  • Deletion timeframe: Upon receiving an account deletion request, user account profiles, authentication records, and synced favorites are permanently deleted from active production databases within 30 days.

7. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All communication between the app and backend services uses industry-standard HTTPS encryption. Authentication tokens are securely stored in protected Android Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences storage.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Control permissions: You can grant, deny, or revoke location permissions at any time via Android Settings (Settings > Apps > SARAN > Permissions).
  • Use without an account: You have the right to use all core transit discovery features anonymously as a guest.
  • Delete bookmarks: You can remove saved places or bookmarks within the app at any time.
  • Request data erasure: You can request complete erasure of your account and associated records by visiting delete-account.html or emailing bijoymandi50@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

SARAN is a general-audience urban transit and navigation application. It is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us at bijoymandi50@gmail.com so we can promptly delete the data.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our transit features, service providers, or regulatory standards. When changes occur, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be highlighted within the application where appropriate.
