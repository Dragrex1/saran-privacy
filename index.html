01 · Introduction

Saran ("Saran", "we", "us", or "our") is a journey-planning application designed to help people find routes across bus, metro, train and ferry transport. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be accessed, collected, used, stored, and disclosed when you use the Saran Android application or contact us. The functionality described here applies only where it is enabled in the version of Saran you use.

1. Information we process

Depending on how you use Saran and which features are enabled, information processed by the app may include:

  • Account and profile information: email address, display name, login identifiers, and any optional profile details you choose to provide, such as date of birth or gender.
  • Authentication information: account verification details, sign-in identifiers and authentication-related records. Passwords, where supported, should be handled as securely hashed credentials rather than stored in plain text.
  • Journey and search information: the origins, destinations, travel preferences, route queries and transit options you select. Some search data may be processed temporarily to generate results.
  • Location information: approximate or precise device location if you choose to grant location permission, as well as places or coordinates you enter manually.
  • Saved places and bookmarks: stop or place names, optional addresses, geographic coordinates and bookmark categories you choose to save.
  • Usage, diagnostic and technical data: app screens and feature interactions, device/app identifiers, operating system or app version, and diagnostics that may be collected by enabled analytics or related SDKs.
  • Support messages: information you submit when contacting us for assistance.

We do not require you to provide optional profile information unless that information is necessary for a feature you choose to use.

2. How we use information

We process relevant information to provide, operate and secure Saran, including to:

  • Register accounts, authenticate sign-ins, and maintain account settings.
  • Search journeys, display transit connections, and provide routing and map features.
  • Show nearby options when you allow device location access.
  • Save and sync bookmarks when account-based saving is enabled.
  • Understand aggregate feature usage, improve app performance, and investigate technical failures.
  • Respond to support or privacy requests and comply with applicable legal duties.

Information is not sold by Saran as a consumer data product. Any use or disclosure by SDK or service providers must be reflected accurately in this policy and in Google Play's Data safety form.

3. Hosting, analytics and service providers

Saran may use the following service providers as part of its technical infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase Analytics: to understand app use, such as screen visits and route-search interactions, when analytics is enabled. Google's handling of information is also governed by its applicable terms and policies.
  • Turso: to host application database records such as user profiles and saved bookmarks when these features are enabled.
  • Map, geocoding, routing, sign-in or other infrastructure providers: if integrated, these services may process the query, coordinates, identifiers or technical data necessary to deliver the requested feature. Before launch, list each actual provider here.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect security or legal rights, or as part of a lawful business transition, subject to applicable protections. We do not treat third-party processing as permission to use information for unrelated purposes.

4. Location and permissions

With your permission, Saran may access your device location to identify nearby stops, support navigation or simplify route planning. You can deny or withdraw permission through Android settings. Where offered, you can also enter your starting point and destination manually.

Using a map or route feature can involve transmitting relevant location coordinates or search terms to the services required to generate a result. Confirm before publication whether Saran stores location history, accesses location in the background, or shares location with a map provider, and describe each practice specifically.

5. Data retention and deletion

We intend to retain account information and saved bookmarks only while needed to operate the account or comply with applicable legal and security requirements. Temporary processing data and analytics may have different retention periods depending on their purpose and the configured provider settings.

If account creation is available, users can request deletion of their Saran account and associated personal data through the in-app deletion function and the external deletion method provided by Saran. Certain records may be retained where legally required or necessary for fraud prevention or security, and those exceptions should be identified in the final published policy.

Before publication: add the real deletion request webpage URL, confirm the in-app deletion flow is implemented, and specify actual retention periods and deletion timeframes. You may contact us at bijoymandi50@gmail.com for privacy-related deletion requests.

6. Data security

We aim to protect information through appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including authenticated access, access restrictions and encrypted network connections where supported. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or data may be at risk, please contact us promptly.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Adjust or revoke app permissions, including location access, in your Android device settings.
  • Review and update account details where editing is supported.
  • Delete saved locations or bookmarks using the available app controls.
  • Request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law.
  • Contact us about privacy concerns, analytics choices or data-processing questions.

Availability of particular rights and controls may depend on your location and applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

Saran's intended audience and age restrictions must match the age groups selected in Google Play Console. If Saran is offered to children, additional protections and disclosures may be required. Before release, replace this paragraph with the correct audience and parental-contact information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Saran's features, service providers or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when we publish a revised version. We may also provide notice in the app when changes materially affect how information is handled.
