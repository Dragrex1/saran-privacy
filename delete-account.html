Account and data requests

Saran can be used without signing in. Account deletion applies only if you chose to create an account. The Delete Account option is available inside Saran, and this page provides an alternative way to request deletion if you no longer have the app.

How to request deletion

  1. Select Request account deletion below to open an email message.
  2. Send the request using the email address associated with your Saran account, if possible.
  3. We may request reasonable verification to help protect your account from unauthorised deletion.

Option 1 — In the app: If you have a Saran account, open the app and use its Delete Account option. Follow the on-screen prompts. The app may require identity verification to protect against unauthorized requests.

Option 2 — Without the app: If you cannot access the app, send an account deletion request to Saran Mobility using the button below. Where possible, send it from the email address associated with your account. We may ask you to verify account ownership.

Request account deletion →

Using Saran as a guest? No account is created merely by using the app without logging in, so there is no Saran account to delete. You can clear local app data through Android Settings. For questions about other data that may be processed during guest use, email bijoymandi50@gmail.com.

What deletion covers

Requests generally concern your account profile, login-linked personal records and saved bookmarks or places associated with your account, where applicable. Saran Mobility will review requests and explain if verification or other action is required.

Data that may be retained

Some records may need to be retained if required by applicable law, to address security incidents or to comply with other legitimate obligations. Third-party analytics records may be subject to separate retention controls. Contact us for information about any applicable retention period.
