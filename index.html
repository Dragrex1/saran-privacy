01 · Introduction

Saran ("Saran", "we", "us", or "our") is a journey-planning application designed to help people find routes across bus, metro, train and ferry transport. This Privacy Policy describes how information is accessed, collected, used, stored and disclosed when you use the Saran Android application or contact us. The functionality described here applies only where it is enabled in the version of Saran you use.

Guest access: You can use Saran without creating an account or signing in. Signing in is optional; account-related information is collected only when you choose to create or use an account. Certain personalized features may work differently for guests.

1. Information we process

Saran supports guest use. The information processed depends on the features you choose to use, whether or not you sign in. It may include:

  • Account and profile information: email address, display name, login identifiers, and any optional profile details you choose to provide, such as date of birth or gender.
  • Authentication information: account verification details, sign-in identifiers and authentication-related records. Authentication credentials are handled by the app’s authentication systems; do not send passwords in support requests.
  • Journey and search information: the origins, destinations, travel preferences, route queries and transit options you select. Some search data may be processed temporarily to generate results.
  • Location information: approximate or precise device location if you choose to grant location permission, as well as places or coordinates you enter manually.
  • Saved places and bookmarks: stop or place names, optional addresses, geographic coordinates and bookmark categories you choose to save.
  • Usage, diagnostic and technical data: app screens and feature interactions, device/app identifiers, operating system or app version, and diagnostics that may be collected by enabled analytics or related SDKs.
  • Support messages: information you submit when contacting us for assistance.

We do not require you to provide optional profile information unless that information is necessary for a feature you choose to use.

2. How we use information

We process relevant information to provide, operate and secure Saran, including to:

  • Register accounts, authenticate sign-ins, and maintain account settings.
  • Search journeys, display transit connections, and provide routing and map features.
  • Show nearby options when you allow device location access.
  • Save and sync bookmarks when account-based saving is enabled.
  • Understand aggregate feature usage, improve app performance, and investigate technical failures.
  • Respond to support or privacy requests and comply with applicable legal duties.

Information is not sold by Saran as a consumer data product. Third-party service providers may process information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

3. Hosting, analytics and service providers

Saran may use the following service providers as part of its technical infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase Analytics: to understand app use, such as screen visits and route-search interactions, when analytics is enabled. Google's handling of information is also governed by its applicable terms and policies.
  • Turso: to host application database records such as user profiles and saved bookmarks when these features are enabled.
  • Map, geocoding, routing, sign-in or other infrastructure services: depending on the features available, these services may process searches, coordinates or technical data necessary to deliver a requested feature.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect security or legal rights, or as part of a lawful business transition, subject to applicable protections. We do not treat third-party processing as permission to use information for unrelated purposes.

4. Location and permissions

With your permission, Saran may access your device location to identify nearby stops, support navigation or simplify route planning. You can deny or withdraw permission through Android settings. Where offered, you can also enter your starting point and destination manually.

5. Data retention and deletion

When you choose to create an account, account information and account-linked bookmarks are retained as needed to provide those features, respond to requests or meet applicable obligations. Guest use does not require an account, although journey requests, device information or analytics may still be processed as described in this policy.

In-app deletion: If you have a Saran account, you can use the Delete Account option provided in the app to initiate account deletion. You can also use our external account deletion request page, including if you no longer have the app installed.

If you have only used Saran as a guest, there is no Saran account to delete. You can remove locally stored app data through your device settings. To request removal of any other personal information associated with your usage, contact bijoymandi50@gmail.com. Certain records may be retained where legally required or necessary for security; analytics data may follow separate provider retention settings.

6. Data security

We use reasonable security measures appropriate to the service, such as access restrictions and secure connections where supported. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or data may be at risk, please contact us promptly.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Adjust or revoke app permissions, including location access, in your Android device settings.
  • Review and update account details where editing is supported.
  • Delete saved locations or bookmarks using the available app controls.
  • Request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law.
  • Contact us about privacy concerns, analytics choices or data-processing questions.

Availability of particular rights and controls may depend on your location and applicable law.

8. Children's privacy

Saran is a general-audience transit and journey-planning application and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent under local law, such as 16 in certain jurisdictions).

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided personal information or created an account without parental consent, please contact us at bijoymandi50@gmail.com. Upon notification, we will take prompt steps to investigate and permanently delete such information from our records.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Saran's features, service providers or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when we publish a revised version. We may also provide notice in the app when changes materially affect how information is handled.
