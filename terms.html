4. Transit Information, Datasets & Attributions

SARAN aggregates public transit information from multiple sources:

  • Official Open GTFS Data: Where available, SARAN integrates publicly published General Transit Feed Specification (GTFS) datasets released under open data initiatives (e.g., Delhi Open Transit Data, TGSRTC, HMRL). Complete attributions, licensing notes, and original publication links are detailed on our Data Sources & Disclaimer page.
  • Independently Compiled Data: For networks without published GTFS feeds (such as regional buses, trams, and river ferries), SARAN independently collects, processes, and maintains timetable records. Such datasets are independently curated and are not government GTFS feeds.
  • Verification Sources: Official railway links (such as indianrail.gov.in and NTES) are provided for independent verification and do not imply any partnership.

For complete source links and operator disclosures, please review our Data Sources & Disclaimer page.

5. Use of Offline Databases

SARAN utilizes local SQLite transit databases stored on your device to support offline route planning in areas with weak cellular coverage. Because these databases are stored locally, they reflect timetable information available at the time the dataset was generated. Operators may adjust schedules, reroute lines, or suspend services before a new database update is released. Users are responsible for updating the app and its database packages periodically.

6. Algorithmic Journey Estimates & No Live Guarantee

All journey durations, transfer walking times, connection recommendations, and fare ranges calculated by SARAN are algorithmic estimates produced by our routing engine. They are not guaranteed departure times or formal operator commitments.

No guaranteed live data: Real-time vehicle arrivals and delay warnings are provided only where supported by active real-time data feeds and reliable network connectivity. Real-world transit operations are subject to traffic congestion, strikes, road repairs, weather disruptions, and unscheduled cancellations.
