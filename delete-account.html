Guest Mode Notice

Using SARAN as a Guest?

No account is created merely by using SARAN. If you use SARAN without signing in, there is no server-side user account or personal profile stored on our servers. You do not need to submit an account deletion request.

To clear locally stored search history, offline cached schedules, or guest bookmarks on your phone, open your Android device settings:

Settings > Apps > SARAN > Storage & cache > Clear Storage / Clear Data

How to Request Account Deletion

If you created an optional account (via Google Sign-In or email registration), you can delete your account using either of the following two methods:

Option 1: In-App Self-Service Deletion

If you currently have the SARAN app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open SARAN on your device.
  2. Tap on Settings or your Profile icon.
  3. Select Account Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt. Your account and cloud-synced bookmarks will be scheduled for permanent removal.

Option 2: External Web-Based Deletion Request (No App Required)

If you uninstalled the application, no longer have access to your Android device, or prefer submitting an external request, you can request account deletion via email without logging into the app:

  1. Click the Submit Account Deletion Request button below.
  2. Send the email from the email address associated with your SARAN account.
  3. Include your registered email address and display name so we can verify account ownership.
  4. Our privacy operations team will verify your ownership and initiate permanent data deletion.

Submit Account Deletion Request →

What Data Is Permanently Deleted

When your account deletion request is processed, the following personal records are permanently erased from our production databases:

  • User account profile: Registered email address, display name, and unique user ID.
  • Authentication credentials: Secure authentication tokens and sign-in mapping records.
  • Synced cloud bookmarks: Saved home, work, and custom favorite transit stops synced to your account profile.
  • User-submitted support history: Any directly linked support tickets or communications, unless required for ongoing legal compliance.

Data That May Be Retained & Exceptions

Certain limited records may be retained temporarily under strict exceptions:

  • Anonymized aggregate telemetry: Fully anonymized statistical counts (such as total app crashes or aggregated feature usage metrics) that cannot be linked to any individual user or email address.
  • Legal and security compliance: Transactional audit logs or records that must be preserved to comply with applicable tax, financial, or statutory legal obligations, or to prevent fraudulent abuse.

Deletion Timeframe

Account deletion requests submitted through our web portal are processed within 30 days of identity verification. Once processed, all personal profile records and synced bookmarks are permanently purged from active databases and will expire from disaster-recovery backups in accordance with standard backup rotation cycles.
